Production and terms

How the shoot runs.

A small, self-sufficient crew already in the field, a defined review process, and a single fee covering production and usage.

The production

  • Photographer plus one assistant, with a skier or snowboarder as talent, or the photographer as the male subject
  • Canon R5C, 45-megapixel stills, delivered as full-resolution print masters
  • Wardrobe to the client's direction: technical alpine, après and lifestyle, or retro ski, in a neutral or high-contrast palette
  • Weather contingency built in: each region offers several shoot days within the window

The schedule

  • On confirmation product shipped to British Columbia; arrival by 18 November keeps the Japan dates available
  • First shoot in the Coast Mountains within the week of product arrival
  • 20 – 30 Nov Hokkaido, including Niseko opening day
  • Within 5 days of each shoot, an edited contact sheet of selects to PULSE
  • From 13 Dec Rockies and Colorado, should a further look be wanted

The terms

  • EUR 8,000 fee, as quoted, covering production and the usage below
  • Worldwide usage, in store and digital, paid and organic, twelve months from first use
  • No category exclusivity
  • Two rounds of selection and retouching feedback included
  • Travel to a client-nominated location available at cost
Approvals and delivery After each shoot, PULSE and Maui Jim receive a contact sheet of edited selects. The client chooses the frames, the photographer grades each in up to three looks, and final print masters are delivered as layered TIFF and high-quality JPEG with embedded colour profiles. The client handles all format adaptations, as set out in the brief.
